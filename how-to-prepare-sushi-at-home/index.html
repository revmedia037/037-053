How to Prepare Sushi at Home

Preparing sushi at home requires fresh ingredients and a bit of practice. Start by cooking sushi rice and seasoning it with rice vinegar, sugar, and salt. Lay a sheet of nori on a bamboo mat, spread a thin layer of rice over it, and add your fillings, such as fresh fish, avocado, and cucumber. Roll the sushi tightly using the bamboo mat, then slice into bite-sized pieces. Serve with soy sauce, wasabi, and pickled ginger.
